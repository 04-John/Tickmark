✓ Tickmark

Books and audit questions for any NEAR treasury, read straight from the chain.

Enter a Sputnik DAO or Trezu treasury. Tickmark reads every payment its proposals made, dates each one by the vote that paid it, values it on that day, places it in a chart of accounts with the reason, checks the balances against the chain, and lists the questions an auditor would ask. Everything runs in your browser from public NEAR data. No account, no key, no server.

Try: daocubator · Near One treasury · BrainDAO

Check a report

Every report is built at one block, and its fingerprint is the SHA-256 of its content. Drop a report.json here: Tickmark rebuilds the books from the chain at that block and compares fingerprints. It also shows any proposal in which the DAO voted on this exact report.

Built at NEAR HACKATHON, November 2026. Open source (MIT): github.com/04-John/tickmark. Chain data: FastNear RPC, archival RPC and transaction index. Prices: CoinGecko daily closes, committed with the code.
